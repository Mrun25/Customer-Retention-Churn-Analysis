Business Context:
This is an e-commerce platform selling multiple product categories.

Problem Statement:
The company wants to understand revenue trends, customer behavior,
and retention to drive growth and profitability.

Key Business Questions:
1. How is revenue trending over time?
2. Who are the most valuable customers?
3. Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
4. Are customers returning after their first purchase?
5. What actionable recommendations can increase revenue?
